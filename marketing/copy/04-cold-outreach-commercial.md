## Outreach hygiene

- Keep first emails under 150 words. Anything longer reads like a pitch.
- Personalize line one of every email. If you can't personalize it, don't send it.
- Don't send Friday afternoon — Tuesday and Wednesday morning convert best in this market.
- Three touches max. After follow-up #2, drop the prospect from sequence — re-add in 90 days.
- Track: open rate (target 30%+), reply rate (target 8%+), meeting booked rate (target 2–3%).
- Bcc Bridgepointefloors@gmail.com on every send so the reply thread is preserved.
