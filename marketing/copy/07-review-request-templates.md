# Review Request Templates

Send within 24 hours of every job. The window between "the work looks great" and "I forgot about it" is short — close it fast.

Three formats for three channels. Plus an in-person script for the final walkthrough.

Replace `[REVIEW LINK]` with your Google Business Profile review shortlink (typically `g.page/r/[ID]/review` — Mark to confirm).

---

## 1. SMS (under 160 characters)

**Template:**

> Hi [FIRST NAME] — Mark with Bridgepointe. Hope the wash looks sharp. If you'd leave a quick Google review, it'd mean a lot: [REVIEW LINK]

**Character count:** 138 (with average-length first name and 25-char shortlink). Stays under the 160-char single-message limit.

**Send time:** Same day, late afternoon (4–6 PM ET). Customer has had time to walk the property and admire the work.

**Variants for repeat customers:**

> Hey [FIRST NAME] — wash #[N] in the books. Quick Google review when you have a sec? [REVIEW LINK] — Mark, Bridgepointe

## 3. Handoff card — printed, left on the kitchen counter

**Format:** 4x6 card, single-sided, charcoal ink on warm-white matte stock. Crew leaves it on the kitchen counter at job sign-off, weighted with a Bridgepointe-branded magnet.

**Front side copy:**

---

**Bridgepointe**
Painting · Cabinet Refinishing · Hot-Water Pressure Washing

---

Thanks for having us out today.

A few things to know:

- **Two-year guarantee** on your cleaning. If mildew comes back inside 24 months, call us — we re-treat free.
- **Concrete dry time:** 4 hours before driving on it.

If you've got 60 seconds and the work looks good — a quick Google review goes a long way. Scan the QR.

> [QR CODE — links to REVIEW LINK]

Questions, anything missed, or you just want us back next year — call **(862) 421-8973**.

— Mark and the Bridgepointe crew
bridgepointepainting.com

---

**Print spec:**
- 4x6 card, 110lb matte cover
- Single charcoal ink color
- QR code printed at 1.25" — high contrast, no logo overlay
- Leave one card per job, every job — no exceptions

## 4. In-person script — final walkthrough (5 lines)

For the crew lead to use during the on-site sign-off, after the walk-around but before pulling the trailer out.

> 1. "Before we head out — does the work look the way you wanted it to?"
> 2. *(Wait for their answer. Address anything they raise. Don't move on until they're satisfied.)*
> 3. "Good. One ask — if you've got 60 seconds tonight, a Google review really does help. The card on the counter has the QR — just scan it."
> 4. "And the two-year guarantee — that's real. If you see mildew come back inside 24 months, call the number on the card. We come back free."
> 5. "Thanks for having us out. Call me direct if anything else comes up — I'm Mark."

**Tone:** Direct. Not asking for a favor — naming a small specific action and giving the reason it matters.

---

## Cadence rules

- SMS goes out same-day, 4–6 PM ET.
- Email goes out next morning, 9–10 AM ET.
- Card is left at job sign-off — not negotiable, every job.
- If no review after 7 days, send one polite reminder text:
  > Hi [FIRST NAME] — Mark again. Just a nudge in case the review link got buried: [REVIEW LINK]. Last note from me on it. Thanks either way.
- If still no review after the reminder — drop it. Don't keep asking.

## Tracking

- Tag every review request in your CRM with the job ID.
- Weekly: review-request-sent vs. review-received ratio. Target 25%+ conversion.
- Monthly: which crew lead generates the highest review rate. The walkthrough script matters — coach to it.
