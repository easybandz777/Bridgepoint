## Bidding + budget notes

- Start at $7 max CPC for exact, $5 for phrase, $3 for broad.
- Daily budget: $50/day to start. Scale once cost per qualified call is below $35.
- Conversion event: any phone call > 60 seconds counted via Google forwarding number.
- Geo-target: 25-mile radius from Buckhead zip 30305. Exclude any zips outside Cobb, Fulton, Gwinnett, DeKalb, Forsyth, Cherokee.
- Day-parting: 7 AM – 7 PM ET, Mon–Sat. Sundays off.
