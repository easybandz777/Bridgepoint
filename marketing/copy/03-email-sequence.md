## Email 2 — Day 7 — The hot-water explainer

**Subject (primary):** Why hot water cleans twice as fast

**Subject alternates:**
- 200°F vs. cold — the math on pressure washing
- The thing most pressure washers won't tell you
- Why your house wash didn't last last time

**Preview text:** Heat does what pressure can't.

**Body:**

Hi [FIRST NAME],

Last week we mentioned the hot-water rig. Here's the actual math behind why it matters.

Cold water at 4,000 PSI moves dirt around. Hot water at 200°F dissolves it. Different physics.

What hot water does that cold water can't:

1. **Kills mildew at the root.** Black streaks on north-facing siding are gloeocapsa — a hardy algae that drinks humidity. Cold water rinses the surface; the spore survives and the streaks come back inside 90 days. Hot water at 200°F sterilizes the spore. That's why we can guarantee mildew-free for 24 months.

2. **Dissolves grease.** Restaurant pads, garage floors, oil-stained driveways — cold water can't pull grease out of porous concrete. Hot water + a degreaser does in one pass what cold water won't do in three.

3. **Cuts cleaning time in half.** A whole-house soft wash that takes a cold-water crew six hours, we do in three. Less time on your property, less water used, less impact on your plants.

The reason most contractors in Atlanta don't run hot water: the rig is expensive, the maintenance is real, and the trailer is heavier. We made the investment because we wanted to put a guarantee on the work — and you can't guarantee what you can't kill.

If you want a free walk-through, we'll show you the spots on your house that need it most.

## Sequence notes

- Day-0 send time: Tue or Thu, 9:30 AM ET. Avoids Monday inbox sweep.
- Day-7 and Day-14: same day-of-week as Day-0 to keep cadence predictable.
- Day-21 (offer): Tue or Wed only. Friday-send hurts urgency on a 14-day countdown.
- Use the customer's first name in the salutation. If first name is blank, fall back to "Hi —" not "Hi [BLANK]."
- Suppress anyone who books inside the sequence — don't keep emailing a customer who already paid.
- Track: open rate (target 35%+), click-through (target 6%+), call attribution (track via mention of "existing-client rate" through Email 4).
