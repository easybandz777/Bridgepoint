# Neighborhood Letter — Drop in Mailboxes Within 1 Block of Every Job

Format: single page, printed two-sided. Front side is the letter (below). Back side is the pricing card from `/marketing/pressure-washing/04-pricing.png`.

Drop within a one-block radius — same side of the street and the immediate cross streets. Walk it the same day the crew finishes. Visible work + paper in the mailbox is the whole point.

Print on warm-white card stock, charcoal ink. No glossy finish — this is a note from a neighbor's contractor, not a flier.

## Fill-in fields (handwrite or pre-print per drop)

- `[ADDRESS]` — house number + street, e.g. "1247 Pine Hill Rd"
- `[SERVICE PERFORMED]` — pull from job ticket
- `[VISIBLE RESULT]` — one specific outcome the neighbor can verify by looking
- `[TIME ON SITE]` — actual hours, e.g. "in and out by lunchtime"
- `[$ AMOUNT]` — actual price paid, rounded to nearest $25

## Print spec

- Stock: 80lb warm-white matte card
- Ink: single-color charcoal (no gold foil for the letter — keep it humble)
- Size: 5x7 or US Letter folded in half
- Quantity per job: 12–20 (one block in each direction)
- Cost: ~$0.40/letter at run quantity

## Drop hygiene

- Don't put it in the mailbox itself if there's a "no soliciting" sticker — door hanger instead.
- Same day as job completion. Twenty-four-hour-old work is half as memorable.
- Crew member walks the block while the trailer reloads — don't pay a dropper.
- Track: which addresses get a follow-up call. Report quarterly: cost per drop vs. revenue per drop.
